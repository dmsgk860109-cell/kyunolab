Privacy Details

Plain site information

Static Site

Kyunolab Mystery Archive is published as a static content site. Readers can browse archive stories, guides, library material, and site notices without logging in.

The site is designed around reading and navigation rather than account-based features.

No Accounts Or Profiles

Kyunolab does not require account registration and does not collect reader-submitted personal information through account forms, profile pages, or comment systems.

If future features ever require reader input, the privacy notice should be updated before those features are treated as public infrastructure.

Third-Party Services

Third-party services such as hosting, analytics, advertising, search engines, browsers, social platforms, or network providers may process standard technical data according to their own policies.

Examples can include IP address, user agent, referral information, approximate location, device data, and interaction signals handled by those services.

Reader Control

Readers can use browser settings, privacy tools, content blockers, platform settings, and search engine controls to manage cookies, scripts, sharing, tracking, and indexing preferences.

Because external services operate under their own policies, readers should review those services directly when they want more detail.
