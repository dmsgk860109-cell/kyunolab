How Kyunolab Reads Strange Material

Calm, clear, and story-aware
BoundariesKnown traditions, modern rumors, source-limited claims, and original retellings should not blur together SourcesWhen a subject is uncertain, the page should make that uncertainty visible instead of hiding it ArchiveStories can be memorable and culturally useful without being presented as confirmed events LibraryCreator material should connect back to archive records rather than floating as isolated content

Editorial posture

Mystery does not need exaggeration to be worth reading.

The archive favors careful phrases, source notes, and visible limits over dramatic certainty. A strange story can stay strange without pretending to be proven.
